Who we are

Helping remove the barriers between students and education.

Bright Futures Education Foundation is a proposed education-support initiative being developed in The Gambia. Our starting point is simple: when families face financial hardship, practical help can make it easier for students to attend school and keep learning.

We aim to work alongside schools, families, local communities, and donors. We will prioritise fairness, student dignity, responsible use of donations, and clear reporting.

Our focus

Practical support for everyday school needs.

Our planned pilot focuses on three costs that can make regular school attendance difficult for families.

📚

School Fees

Help eligible students with verified school-fee costs, subject to funds available and agreed arrangements with schools.

🚌

Transport

Contribute toward the cost of travelling to school so that distance and daily transport expenses are less of a barrier.

🍲

Lunch Support

Help students meet lunch costs during the school day, supporting their ability to participate in learning.

Our first step

A focused pilot in four communities.

The proposed pilot is designed to begin on a manageable scale, learn from school and community partners, and report clearly on what the support achieves.

Trust matters

Accountability from the beginning.

We want supporters to understand how decisions are made and how contributions are handled as the initiative develops.

How we plan to work

  • Use consistent criteria to assess applications.
  • Verify relevant school costs with appropriate permission.
  • Keep records of donations, spending, and decisions.
  • Protect children’s privacy and dignity.
  • Provide donors with clear project and financial updates.

What we will confirm before launch

  • Applicable registration and governance requirements.
  • School partnerships and verified student needs.
  • Funds available and the final programme budget.
  • Safe, transparent methods for delivering assistance.
